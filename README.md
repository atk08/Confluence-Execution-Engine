# Confluence Execution Engine (CEE)

## Vision

The Confluence Execution Engine is the execution layer for Confluence Scanner AI.

Rather than producing traditional buy/sell signals, CEE identifies institutional trading zones by combining:

- Volume Profile
- Point of Control (POC)
- High Volume Nodes (HVNs)
- Low Volume Nodes (LVNs)
- Anchored VWAP
- Fair Value Gaps (FVGs)
- Relative Volume
- Market Regime Analysis

These factors are combined into a transparent Confluence Score and explained using an Auction Story that tells traders why a setup exists.

---

## Philosophy

Markets move because of auctions.

Indicators do not move price.

Institutions leave footprints through volume, acceptance, rejection, and imbalance.

CEE identifies those footprints.

---

## Core Principles

- Score locations, not assets.
- Every score must be explainable.
- Confluence beats individual indicators.
- Simplicity over chart clutter.
- Every trade must tell an auction story.
