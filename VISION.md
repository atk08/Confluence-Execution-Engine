# Confluence Scanner AI

## Mission

Build the world's most explainable institutional market analysis engine.

The objective is not to predict markets.

The objective is to identify high-quality institutional trading locations through measurable evidence.

Every decision made by the engine must be:

- Objective
- Explainable
- Testable
- Repeatable
- Research Driven

---

## Vision

Create a platform capable of analyzing any financial market using institutional evidence.

Supported markets include:

- Crypto
- Stocks
- ETFs
- Forex
- Futures

The engine should continuously improve through research and historical validation.

---

## Principles

We do not score indicators.

We score evidence.

We do not hide our reasoning.

We explain every score.

We do not optimize for complexity.

We optimize for clarity.

We do not rely on opinion.

We rely on measurable results.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the reference platform for institutional confluence analysis.
