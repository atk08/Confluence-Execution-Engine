Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

The Scoring Model defines how the Confluence Engine converts institutional evidence into an Institutional Confidence Score (ICS).

The objective is to produce a score that is:

- Objective
- Explainable
- Repeatable
- Testable
- Adaptable

No score may rely on subjective interpretation.

Every point awarded by the engine must be traceable to measurable market evidence.

---

# Core Philosophy

The engine does not score indicators.

The engine scores evidence.

Evidence is grouped into Confluence Zones.

Confluence Zones are evaluated within the current Market Context.

The Institutional Confidence Engine then determines the final Institutional Confidence Score.
