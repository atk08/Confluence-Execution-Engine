# Research Framework

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

The Research Framework exists to validate every assumption made by the Confluence Engine.

No scoring rule should remain permanent without statistical evidence supporting its effectiveness.

The objective is continuous improvement through measurable results rather than opinion.

---

# Philosophy

Every scoring factor begins as a hypothesis.

Examples:

- Untouched POCs outperform revisited POCs.
- Anchored VWAP alignment improves reaction probability.
- Fresh Fair Value Gaps increase setup quality.
- Strong Relative Volume increases follow-through.
- Institutional Alignment improves trade expectancy.

The purpose of research is to determine whether these hypotheses are true, false, or require adjustment.

---

# Research Cycle

Hypothesis

↓

Historical Testing

↓

Performance Analysis

↓

Weight Adjustment

↓

Validation

↓

Production

↓

Continuous Monitoring

---

# Core Principles

- Every hypothesis must be testable.
- Every result must be reproducible.
- Every engine revision must be measurable.
- No scoring adjustment should rely solely on intuition.

---

# Data Collection

Each historical setup should store:

- Symbol
- Asset Class
- Timeframe
- Timestamp
- Market Context
- Evidence Objects
- Evidence Scores
- Confluence Strength
- Institutional Confidence Score
- Entry Price
- Stop Loss
- Target
- Outcome
- Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE)
- Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE)

This creates a complete historical dataset for analysis.

---

# Performance Metrics

The framework evaluates:

- Win Rate
- Average Reward/Risk
- Profit Factor
- Expectancy
- Maximum Drawdown
- Average Holding Time
- Reaction Quality
- Follow-Through Distance

---

# Validation Rules

No scoring change enters production until:

- Historical testing is complete.
- Performance improves meaningfully.
- Results are consistent across multiple market conditions.
- Results are reproducible.

---

# Version Control

Every research result is linked to:

- Engine Version
- Scoring Model Version
- Research Dataset Version

Historical results must always remain reproducible.
