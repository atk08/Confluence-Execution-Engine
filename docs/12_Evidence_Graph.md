# Evidence Graph

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

The Evidence Graph models the relationships between institutional evidence.

Rather than evaluating indicators independently, the engine evaluates how evidence reinforces or contradicts other evidence.

The graph becomes the structural representation of market confluence.

---

# Nodes

Each Evidence Object is represented as a node.

Examples:

- Primary POC
- Composite POC
- Developing POC
- HVN
- LVN
- VAH
- VAL
- Anchored VWAP
- Bullish FVG
- Bearish FVG
- Relative Volume

---

# Relationships

Evidence may form relationships including:

- Aligns With
- Confirms
- Strengthens
- Weakens
- Inside
- Outside
- Above
- Below
- Touching
- Overlapping

Relationships are evaluated by the Confluence Zone Engine.

---

# Graph Objective

The objective is to determine how strongly institutional evidence agrees around a price location.

The greater the agreement between high-quality evidence objects, the stronger the resulting Confluence Zone.
