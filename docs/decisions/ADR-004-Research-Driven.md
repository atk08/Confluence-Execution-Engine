# ADR-004: Research-Driven Development

Status: Accepted

Date: July 2026

## Decision

Every mathematical formula is considered a hypothesis until validated.

Historical testing determines whether scoring rules should remain, change, or be removed.

## Rationale

Trading systems should evolve based on measurable evidence rather than opinion.

## Consequences

The Research Framework is responsible for validating every formula.

Production weights may only change following successful validation.
